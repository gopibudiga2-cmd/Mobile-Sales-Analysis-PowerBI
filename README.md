📱 Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview

The Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze mobile sales performance, customer ratings, transaction volumes, payment methods, and brand-wise sales. It helps businesses understand sales trends and make data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall mobile sales performance.
Track total sales, total quantity, average sales, and total transactions.
Identify top-performing mobile brands and cities.
Understand customer ratings and payment preferences.
Monitor daily, monthly, quarterly, and yearly sales trends.
Compare current sales with the same period last year.
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development and visualization
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX – Calculated measures and time-intelligence analysis
Data Visualization – Interactive charts, KPI cards, and maps
Microsoft Excel / CSV – Data source, if applicable
📊 Dashboard Pages
1. Mobile Sales Overview

Provides a summary of overall mobile sales performance.

Key Metrics:

Total Sales
Total Quantity Sold
Average Sales
Total Transactions

Visualizations:

Total Sales by City
Total Quantity by Month
Customer Ratings by Rating Status
Transactions by Payment Method
Total Sales by Brand
Total Sales by Day Name
2. Month-to-Date (MTD) Analysis

Analyzes sales performance throughout the selected month.

Key Features:

Daily MTD sales trends
Cumulative sales performance
Interactive month selection
KPI cards for sales, quantity, average sales, and transactions
3. Same Period Last Year Analysis

Compares current sales performance with the corresponding previous-year period.

Key Features:

Year-wise sales comparison
Quarter-wise sales analysis
Month-wise sales comparison
Current sales versus same-period-last-year sales
📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Sales: Overall sales amount
Total Quantity: Total quantity of mobile products sold
Average Sales: Average sales value, based on the report's measure definition
Total Transactions: Number of sales transactions
MTD Sales: Month-to-date sales
Same Period Last Year: Sales for the corresponding period in the previous year
